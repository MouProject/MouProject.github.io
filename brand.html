Mou identity

The Mou programming language

Developed by HAZOS.

The project uses a yellow M symbol and the Mou / Moulang names to identify its official materials. The Brand repository is the central location for artwork and licensing notices.

Official logo guidance

Use the project name accurately when discussing Mou. Keep the official logo recognizable, with its original proportions and colors, and clearly distinguish community projects from official releases.

Do not imply that an independent product is endorsed by HAZOS or the Mou Project. For logo reuse, modifications or merchandise beyond rights already available under applicable law or an existing license, contact the project.

Policy status: Detailed logo permissions are still being finalized. These are identification guidelines, not a new copyright license or a claim of registered trademark rights.
